Dumpling Lasagna

Description

This is the viral dumpling lasagna that I will definitely be making again. It's basically like a giant dumpling and saves so much time because you don't have to fold so many dumplings at once.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb / 450g ground pork (30% fat, 70% lean)
  • 1 Tbsp plus 1 tsp cornstarch
  • 1 Tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 Tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 Tbsp Shaoxing wine
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp ground white pepper
  • 4 scallions, green parts only, thinly sliced
  • 1 egg
  • 1 (1-inch / 2.5cm) piece ginger, minced
  • 1⁄2 cup / 120ml water
  • Fly By Jing Original Sichuan Chili Crisp

Instructions

  1. cube watermelon
  2. cube watermelon
  3. Squeeze lime over the watermelon on a serving platter
  4. Sprinkle with flakey sea salt
  5. Add crumbled feta or goat cheese
  6. Drizzle with Fly By Jing Original Sichuan Chili Crisp and serve!
