Mapo Tofu Noodles

Description

What could make the legendary spicy, tingly Sichuan dish Mapo Tofu (麻婆豆腐, mápó dòufu) even better? Serving it on top of sun-dried, knife cut squiggly noodles. This flavorful, hearty weeknight meal comes together in 25 minutes but feels like you're at your favorite restaurant.

Ingredients

  • Chili oil
  • Noodles
  • Scalions
  • Water

Instructions

  1. Make Chef Jing's Mapo Tofu
  2. While that cooks, boil a batch of Chili Crisp Noodles. They're done in just 6 minutes and come out bouncy every time.
  3. Top your noodles with the mapo tofu, garnish with scallions, and dig in.
