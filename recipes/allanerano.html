Noodles Alla nerano

Description

Spaghetti alla Nerano gets a Sichuan Glow-up. As the mom of a toddler I'm always looking for easy ways to put dinner on the page. I saw this viral noodle recipe all over my FYP and decided to make it even easier. Ready in less than 10 minutes, a 12g of protein meal is a deliciously easy way to use summer's zucchini harvest. Use Fly By Jing Roasted Garlic Noodles for the rich umami roasted garlic sauce.

Ingredients

  • Chili oil
  • Noodles
  • Garlic
  • Parmesan

Instructions

  1. Boil Fly By Jing Noodles for 5 minutes so they are al dente.
  2. Slice zucchini thin and saute in olive oil and Garlic Sauce packet until golden.
  3. Toss hot noodles with your zucchini, fresh basil, grated Parmesan, and bit of lemon zest.
