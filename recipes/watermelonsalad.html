Watermelon salad

Description

This viral recipe is made for hot August days. Whether you’re hosting or trying to stay cool, this comes together in under 10 minutes and is a showstopper. Fresh juicy watermelon is drizzled with lime and salt spicy, crunchy Sichuan Chili Crisp for a complex, craveable salad.

Ingredients

  • Chili oil
  • Watermelon
  • Lime
  • Goat cheese

Instructions

  1. cube watermelon
  2. cube watermelon
  3. Squeeze lime over the watermelon on a serving platter
  4. Sprinkle with flakey sea salt
  5. Add crumbled feta or goat cheese
  6. Drizzle with Fly By Jing Original Sichuan Chili Crisp and serve!
